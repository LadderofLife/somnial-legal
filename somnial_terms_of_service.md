Somnial is a mobile application that allows users to:
- Generate original, personalised short stories using artificial intelligence based on user-selected parameters (genre, mood, ending type, story length, and language difficulty)

Story content is generated by Anthropic PBC's Claude AI model via our secure proxy server. Each story is uniquely generated in real time based on your inputs.
